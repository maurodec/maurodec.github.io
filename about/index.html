About

Hi, I’m Mauro, a software engineer from Uruguay 🇺🇾. I lived in Dublin 🇮🇪 for the better part of a decade, and eventually moved to Valencia 🇪🇸. I’m a foodie, a gamer, and a passionate programmer.

The purpose of this simple blog is for me to write down things I might find interesting or that I might want to refer back to in the future. Time capsule purposes aside, I want a place to write down my thoughts and share them with whomever might care to read them.
